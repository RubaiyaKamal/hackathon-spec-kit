You don't need thousands of dollars to start building robots. Here's how to build capable robots for under $100.

## Project 1: Line-Following Robot ($30-40)

**What It Does**: Follows black line on white surface

**Parts List**:
- Arduino Nano clone: $3
- L298N motor driver: $2
- 2x DC motors with wheels: $5
- IR sensors (5x): $3
- Battery holder (4xAA): $2
- Chassis (cardboard or 3D print): $0-5
- Jumper wires: $2
- Batteries: $3

**Total**: ~$20-25

**Skills Learned**:
- Basic electronics
- Sensor reading
- Motor control
- PID control (optional)

**Build Time**: 4-6 hours

**Difficulty**: Beginner

## Money-Saving Tips

**1. Buy from AliExpress/Banggood**
- 50-70% cheaper than Amazon
- Wait time: 2-4 weeks
- Quality varies (read reviews)

**2. Use Arduino Clones**
- $3 vs $25 for official
- Functionally identical
- Support open-source later

**3. 3D Print Parts**
- Free designs on Thingiverse
- Library/makerspace access
- Or use cardboard!

**4. Salvage Components**
- Old toys for motors
- Broken electronics for parts
- Cardboard for chassis

**5. Buy Kits**
- Often cheaper than individual parts
- Includes instructions
- Good for beginners

**6. Share Costs**
- Build with friends
- Split component packs
- Share tools

## Common Mistakes to Avoid

❌ **Buying expensive parts first**: Start cheap, upgrade later
❌ **Overcomplicating**: Simple robots teach more
❌ **Not testing incrementally**: Test each component
❌ **Skipping documentation**: Take notes, photos
❌ **Giving up too early**: Debugging is learning
