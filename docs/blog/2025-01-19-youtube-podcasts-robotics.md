Stay updated and learn from the best with these essential YouTube channels and podcasts for robotics.

## Top YouTube Channels

### Boston Dynamics
**Focus**: Cutting-edge humanoid and quadruped robots
**Why Watch**: See the state-of-the-art in action
**Best For**: Inspiration, understanding what's possible
**Frequency**: Monthly uploads

### Two Minute Papers
**Focus**: AI and robotics research explained simply
**Why Watch**: Latest research papers summarized
**Best For**: Staying current with research
**Frequency**: 2-3 times per week

### Lex Fridman
**Focus**: Long-form interviews with robotics/AI leaders
**Why Watch**: Deep insights from experts
**Notable Guests**: Elon Musk, Yann LeCun, Rodney Brooks
**Best For**: Understanding the field deeply

### Simone Giertz

**Why Watch**: Entertaining, shows the joy of building

## How to Learn Effectively

**Active Watching**:
- Take notes
- Pause and implement concepts
- Try to predict solutions before revealed

**Build Along**:
- Follow tutorial projects
- Modify and experiment
- Share your results

**Community Engagement**:
- Comment with questions
- Join Discord/Reddit communities
- Share what you learned

## Staying Updated

**Subscribe to**:
- 5-10 channels you genuinely enjoy
- 2-3 podcasts for commutes

**Set Aside Time**:
- 30 minutes daily for videos
- 1 hour weekly for podcasts

**Don't Overwhelm**:
- Quality over quantity
- Focus on channels that match your interests
