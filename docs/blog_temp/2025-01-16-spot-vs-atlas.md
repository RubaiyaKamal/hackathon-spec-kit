Boston Dynamics has two iconic robots: Spot (quadruped) and Atlas (humanoid). Here's how they compare and what each excels at.

## Spot: The Quadruped

**Form Factor**: Four-legged robot dog
**Weight**: 70 lbs (32 kg)
**Payload**: 31 lbs (14 kg)
**Battery Life**: 90 minutes
**Price**: ~$75,000

**Key Capabilities**:
- Navigate rough terrain
- Climb stairs
- Open doors
- Carry payloads
- 360° vision

**Commercial Status**: Available for purchase since 2020

## Atlas: The Humanoid

**Form Factor**: Bipedal humanoid
**Height**: 5'9" (175 cm)
**Weight**: 196 lbs (89 kg)
**Power**: Tethered or battery
**Price**: Not for sale (research platform)

**Key Capabilities**:
- Parkour and backflips
- Object manipulation
- Dynamic balance
- Running and jumping

**Commercial Status**: Research only, not commercially available

## Head-to-Head Comparison

### Mobility
**Spot**: ★★★★★
- Stable on four legs
- Excellent on rough terrain
- Reliable locomotion

**Atlas**: ★★★★☆
- More agile and dynamic
- Can do parkour
- Less stable (bipedal)

**Winner**: Spot for reliability, Atlas for agility

### Manipulation
**Spot**: ★★★☆☆
- Arm attachment available
- Limited dexterity
- Good for simple tasks

**Atlas**: ★★★★★
- Human-like hands
- Complex manipulation
- Can use tools

**Winner**: Atlas

### Practical Deployment
**Spot**: ★★★★★
- Proven in real-world use
- Hundreds deployed
- Reliable and robust

**Atlas**: ★★☆☆☆
- Research platform
- Not ready for deployment
- Impressive but impractical (currently)

**Winner**: Spot

### Cost-Effectiveness
**Spot**: ★★★★☆
- $75K is expensive but justifiable
- ROI proven in inspections
- Lower maintenance

**Atlas**: ★☆☆☆☆
- Not for sale
- Would be extremely expensive
- High maintenance

**Winner**: Spot

## Final Thoughts

Spot and Atlas represent different approaches to robotics. Spot is practical, reliable, and commercially successful. Atlas is ambitious, impressive, and aspirational.

Both are engineering marvels. Spot shows what's possible today. Atlas shows what might be possible tomorrow.
