Tesla's Optimus humanoid robot is one of the most ambitious robotics projects ever. Here's everything you need to know about its capabilities, limitations, and potential impact.

## What is Optimus?

**Official Name**: Tesla Bot / Optimus
**Announced**: August 2021 (AI Day)
**First Prototype**: September 2022
**Latest Version**: Optimus Gen 2 (December 2023)

**Goal**: General-purpose humanoid robot for repetitive, dangerous, or boring tasks.

## Technical Specifications (Gen 2)

**Physical**:
- Height: 5'8" (173 cm)
- Weight: 121 lbs (55 kg) - lighter than Gen 1
- Degrees of Freedom: 28 (11 per hand)

**Performance**:
- Walking Speed: 5 mph (8 km/h)
- Carrying Capacity: 45 lbs (20 kg)
- Dead Weight Lift: 150 lbs (68 kg)

**Power**:
- Battery: 2.3 kWh (full day of work)
- Charging: Standard outlet

## Key Capabilities

### 1. Dexterity
**Hands**: 11 degrees of freedom per hand
**Tactile Sensing**: Force sensors in fingers
**Precision**: Can handle delicate objects (eggs, small parts)

**Demonstrated**:
- Sorting colored blocks
- Picking up objects of various shapes
- Gentle manipulation

### 2. Mobility
**Walking**: Stable bipedal locomotion
**Balance**: Can recover from pushes
**Terrain**: Flat surfaces, stairs (in development)

**Improvements from Gen 1**:
- 30% faster walking
- Smoother gait
- Better balance

### 3. Perception
**Sensors**:
- 8 cameras (similar to Tesla vehicles)
- Force/torque sensors
- IMU (Inertial Measurement Unit)

**Vision System**: Same neural networks as Tesla FSD (Full Self-Driving)

### 4. Intelligence
**AI**: Tesla's neural network stack
**Learning**: Can learn tasks through demonstration
**Adaptation**: Adjusts to different environments

## What Makes Optimus Different?

**1. Automotive Manufacturing Expertise**
- Tesla knows mass production
- Cost optimization
- Supply chain at scale

**2. AI Infrastructure**
- Leverages Tesla's massive AI training infrastructure
- Same vision system as cars (proven at scale)
- Continuous improvement via fleet learning

**3. Vertical Integration**
- Designs own actuators, electronics, software
- Controls entire stack
- Can optimize for cost and performance

**4. Target Price**
- Elon's Goal: <$20,000 per unit
- Mass production could achieve this
- Would be revolutionary for pricing

## Current Limitations

**What It Can't Do (Yet)**:
- Complex manipulation tasks
- Navigate unstructured environments reliably
- Operate fully autonomously for extended periods
- Handle unexpected situations well

**Challenges**:
- Sim-to-real gap (simulation ≠ reality)
- Edge cases are infinite
- Safety certification
- Public acceptance
